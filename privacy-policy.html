01 Introduction

Viewora is a social platform for short videos, long videos, posts, stories, messaging, and live experiences. By using Viewora, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

In short: We collect information needed to run the product (account, content, device, usage), use it to provide and improve Viewora, and share it only as described here — not by selling your personal data.

02 Information we collect

We collect information in these categories:

Account & profile

  • Name, username, email, phone (if provided), password or auth provider data
  • Profile photo, bio, links, verification status, subscription tier
  • Followers / following relationships

Content you create

  • Posts, photos, videos, shorts, stories, highlights, captions, hashtags
  • Comments, likes, reactions, saves, shares
  • Music, stickers, effects, and other creative tools you apply

Messages & communications

  • Direct messages, voice notes, media you send in chat
  • Call metadata (time, duration, participants) for voice/video calls

Usage & device

  • App interactions, watch history signals, search queries, notifications opened
  • Device type, OS, browser, IP address, language, crash logs, performance data
  • Approximate location derived from IP (not precise GPS unless you enable it)

Payments

  • Subscription plan, purchase history, limited payment status from providers such as Razorpay
  • We do not store full card numbers on Viewora servers

03 How we use information

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the Services (feed ranking, recommendations, search)
  • Enable messaging, calls, stories, uploads, and social features
  • Process subscriptions, monetization eligibility, and creator tools
  • Show badges (blue / white / red ticks) and verification status
  • Send service notifications (likes, comments, messages, security alerts)
  • Detect spam, abuse, fraud, and enforce our policies
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests
  • Analyze product performance in aggregated or de-identified form

04 How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Other users — when you post publicly, comment, follow, or message someone
  • Service providers — cloud hosting (e.g. Firebase), media storage/CDN, analytics, payment processors, under contracts that protect data
  • Legal & safety — when required by law, or to protect Viewora, users, or the public from harm
  • Business transfers — if we merge, acquire, or sell assets, data may transfer under continued protection
We do not sell your personal information for money. We also do not share your private messages for advertising.

05 Your content & public information

Content you set to public can be seen by anyone using Viewora (and may be reshared or downloaded by others). Profile information such as username, photo, bio, and follower counts is typically visible to other users.

You can control visibility for some posts and videos (public / private where available). Stories may disappear after 24 hours unless saved as highlights.

06 Messages & calls

Direct messages and call features are intended for private communication between participants. Viewora may access message content only in limited cases such as:

  • Technical delivery and storage of the service
  • Safety investigations when content is reported
  • Legal obligations

End-to-end encryption may not apply to all message types. Do not share passwords or sensitive financial secrets in chat.

07 Payments & subscriptions

Paid plans (Plus, Pro, Elite, etc.) are processed by third-party payment providers (for example, Razorpay). Those providers process card/UPI details under their own privacy policies. Viewora receives confirmation of payment status, plan type, and billing cycle needed to unlock features and badges.

08 Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and authenticated APIs to protect data. No system is 100% secure. Please use a strong password, enable device security, and report suspicious activity.

09 Data retention

We keep information as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Services. If you request account deletion, we process the request (including admin review where required). After approval, we delete or de-identify personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain records for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Backups may take additional time to fully purge. Public content you shared may remain with other users who saved or reshared it.

10 Your rights & choices

Depending on your region, you may have rights to:

  • Access or download a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccurate profile information
  • Delete your account (see Delete account)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Manage notification preferences in-app
  • Control who can message or see certain content (where available)

To exercise rights, use in-app settings or contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

11 Children’s privacy

Viewora is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12 International users

Viewora may process and store information on servers located in different countries (including via Firebase / Google Cloud and other providers). By using the Services, you understand your information may be transferred to countries with different data-protection laws. We take steps designed to protect data in line with this Policy.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, provide additional notice (in-app or by email where appropriate). Continued use of Viewora after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
