Community Guidelines

Help keep Viewora safe and creative for everyone.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or targeted attacks
  • No threats of violence or glorifying harm
  • Disagree without personal attacks

Keep it legal & safe

  • No illegal activity, scams, or fraud
  • No sexual content involving minors — zero tolerance
  • No non-consensual intimate imagery
  • No graphic violence used to shock or threaten

Authenticity

  • No impersonation of people or brands
  • No fake engagement, bots, or view farms
  • Misinformation that can cause real-world harm may be limited

Spam & platform abuse

  • No mass unsolicited DMs or comment spam
  • No malware links or phishing
  • No attempts to bypass bans or security

Intellectual property

Only post content you have rights to. Music, clips, and images you don’t own can be removed. See Copyright.

Enforcement

We may remove content, limit features, or suspend accounts. Serious or repeated violations can lead to permanent ban. Report issues via Report.

Tip: When in doubt, don’t post it. Creativity thrives when people feel safe.
