Community Guidelines

Viewora is for creating, watching, sharing and connecting. These rules keep the platform safe for everyone — including teens 13+ and creators.

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to all accounts, posts, shorts, stories, live, chat and profiles.

Spirit Age Respect Safety Content Spam IP Report Enforcement

1. The spirit of Viewora

We want a place where people can express themselves, grow audiences, and discover videos — without fear of harm, scams, or abuse.

Do: be authentic, credit others, disagree politely, report problems early.
Don’t: harass, exploit, scam, or post illegal or dangerous material.

2. Age requirement (13+)

You must be at least 13 years old to create or use a Viewora account.

  • We may ask for date of birth or age confirmation at signup.
  • Accounts of under-13 users will be removed when we become aware of them.
  • Parents/guardians: contact us if a child under 13 has an account.
Sexual content involving minors (anyone 17 or under) is strictly forbidden — including fictional, AI-generated, or “roleplay” depictions. We report illegal child sexual abuse material to authorities where required.

3. Respect & harassment

Do not target people with abuse. Prohibited behaviour includes:

  • Threats of violence, death threats, or encouraging self-harm
  • Bullying, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, or doxxing (sharing private info)
  • Hate speech based on race, religion, gender, orientation, disability, caste, or nationality
  • Sexual harassment, unsolicited sexual content, or non-consensual intimate imagery
  • Impersonation of another person or brand in a misleading way

4. Dangerous & illegal activity

Content or behaviour that promotes real-world harm is not allowed, including:

  • Terrorism, violent extremism, or organized crime recruitment
  • Instructions for weapons, explosives, or violent crime
  • Drug trafficking, scams, fraud, phishing, or stolen payment data
  • Human trafficking, exploitation, or coercion
  • Graphic real violence shared for shock or glorification
News, education, or documentary context may be allowed when clearly labelled and not instructional for harm. We still remove content that crosses the line into promotion or how-to guidance for crime.

5. Content standards (posts, shorts, stories, live)

Adult / sexual content: Consensual adult content may be restricted or labelled. Non-consensual, abusive, or illegal sexual content is banned.

Nudity: Follow local law. Sexual content involving minors is never allowed.

Violence & gore: Extreme gore or animal cruelty for entertainment is not allowed.

Misinformation: Don’t run coordinated campaigns that cause harm (e.g. health or election fraud that can cause real damage).

Live: Hosts are responsible for their stream. Ban evasion and ban-dodging accounts may be removed.

6. Spam, bots & manipulation

  • No mass follow/unfollow, fake engagement, or bought metrics
  • No malware links, phishing, or deceptive “giveaway” scams
  • No automated accounts that spam comments, DMs, or posts
  • Don’t flood feeds with repetitive promotional content

7. Intellectual property

Only post content you own or have the right to use (music, clips, logos, photos).

  • Copyright owners may request removal of infringing material.
  • Repeated infringement can lead to account termination.
  • Fair use / commentary is not a free pass to re-upload full works.

8. How to report

Use in-app Report on a profile, post, short, story, live, or chat when available.

You can also reach us via the contact method listed in Privacy Policy / Settings → About.

Reports are reviewed as capacity allows. Urgent safety issues (imminent harm, child exploitation) are prioritised.

9. Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, we may:

  • Remove content or limit distribution
  • Warn, restrict features, or temporary suspend
  • Permanently ban accounts and related devices where abuse continues
  • Preserve and disclose data when required by law
